About VisionAid

VisionAid is an advanced AI and Machine Learning project specifically designed for visually impaired individuals. It utilizes cutting-edge YOLOv8 object detection paired with a mobile IP camera or wearable device (like a smart watch) to perceive the surrounding environment in real-time. By processing this visual data instantly, VisionAid provides rapid auditory feedback through connected earbuds or directly from the device, announcing the names of detected objects and potential obstacles. This enables greater independence, spatial awareness, and safety in navigating daily life.
